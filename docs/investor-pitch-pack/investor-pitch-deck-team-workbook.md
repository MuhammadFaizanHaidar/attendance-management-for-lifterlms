## 14. Red Flags to Fix Before the Deck Is Final

- unclear first customer
- weak proof
- vague ROI
- too many features
- no clear wedge
- unsupported market claims
- generic AI story without workflow depth

---

## 15. Sign-off Checklist

Before the deck is finalized, confirm:

- product vision approved
- Phase 1 scope approved
- customer profile approved
- proof points approved
- market logic approved
- pricing assumptions approved
- team story approved
- fundraising story approved
