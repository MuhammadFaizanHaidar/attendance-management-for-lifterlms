AI Work Verification and Auto-Invoicing Platform

Strategic Product Document

Build a platform that cross-verifies work completed against hours logged, compares delivery effort against internal and later market benchmarks, validates invoices, and auto-generates invoice drafts from approved work records.

Prepared for team alignment, planning, and sharing with AI/copilot systems.

1. Executive Summary

This product will help service teams answer three important questions:

  1. Was the logged work supported by real delivery evidence?
  2. Was the time and cost reasonable compared to similar internal work and future market benchmarks?
  3. Can that verified work flow into faster, more accurate invoice creation?

The fastest route to value is a tight first release focused on PM tools, time tracking, verification logic, and invoice draft generation. Wider AI analysis and deeper benchmarking can then expand during beta and after customer feedback.

Primary Problem

Hours are logged in one tool, delivery happens in another, and invoices are created elsewhere. This creates weak visibility, billing disputes, and poor benchmark intelligence.

Initial Solution

Connect PM, time, and accounting systems; verify hours against work signals; flag anomalies; and generate invoice drafts from approved records.

2. Product Vision

Working statement: An AI-assisted work verification and invoicing platform that turns task activity, time logs, and business records into verified, benchmarked, invoice-ready output.

  • Reduce unsupported or inflated time entries
  • Improve billing confidence
  • Speed up invoice preparation
  • Reduce disputes and manual reconciliation
  • Create a long-term benchmark layer across teams and projects

3. First Target Users

  • Agencies
  • Software consultancies
  • Professional services teams
  • Delivery, operations, and finance leaders inside service-based businesses

4. Core Product Capabilities

Capability What it does
Work Verification Links time entries to tasks and supporting activity, then scores whether work appears sufficiently evidenced.
Benchmarking Compares effort and cost to similar internal work first, then later to external market norms.
Invoice Validation Checks invoice lines, rates, users, and hours against approved work and system records.
Auto-Invoicing Creates invoice drafts from approved users, tasks, hours, and rate card rules.

5. Phase-by-Phase Delivery Plan

PHASE 1 - FASTEST RELEASE / MVP

Main Goal

Ship a focused product quickly and get into beta as fast as possible.

Phase 1 Product Promise

Connect your PM and time systems, verify whether logged work is supported by delivery activity, and generate invoice drafts from approved records.

Scope

  • PM integration: Jira or ClickUp
  • Time-tracking integration: Harvest or Tempo
  • Accounting integration: QuickBooks or Xero
  • Optional support signal: GitHub
  • Verification dashboard
  • Invoice draft generator
  • Review and approval flow

Must-have Features

  1. Organization/workspace setup
  2. User identity mapping across systems
  3. Project, task, user, and time entry sync
  4. Rules-based work verification
  5. Verification dashboard for flagged and approved records
  6. Invoice draft generation from approved hours
  7. Shareable invoice support summary

AI in Phase 1

  • Summarize verified work for invoice descriptions
  • Explain why a time entry was flagged
  • Help normalize unclear task descriptions

Out of Scope

  • Deep email parsing
  • Invoice OCR
  • External market benchmarking
  • Complex ERP workflows
  • Fully automated invoice sending
Why this phase first: It is the fastest path to real customer value, clean beta feedback, and a strong product story without overbuilding too early.

Phase 1 Exit Criteria

  • A customer can connect tools successfully
  • A manager can review verified versus flagged hours
  • A finance user can generate a draft invoice from approved work
  • The system explains why entries were flagged

PHASE 2 - BETA EXPANSION

Main Goal

Increase trust, validation depth, and real-world coverage while early customers actively use the MVP.

Scope

  • Gmail and Outlook integration
  • Invoice PDF upload
  • OCR/document extraction
  • Cross-check invoice lines against approved work
  • Basic internal benchmarking by task type, role, and project
  • Exception review queue

Beta Focus

  • Accuracy of verification scores
  • Usefulness of discrepancy flags
  • Cross-system mapping quality
  • Finance team confidence in draft invoices

Exit Criteria

  • Beta teams trust the system enough to review it regularly
  • Invoice validation catches real mismatches
  • Internal benchmark views are useful for decision-making
PHASE 3 - INTELLIGENCE AND BENCHMARKING

Main Goal

Move from operational verification into delivery intelligence and performance analysis.

  • Historical benchmarking engine
  • Cross-team and cross-project comparisons
  • Role and project-level productivity trends
  • Effort variance analysis
  • Margin and efficiency insights
PHASE 4 - MARKET BENCHMARKING AND ADVANCED AUTOMATION

Main Goal

Add external market context and deeper automation once internal data quality and auditability are strong.

  • External market rate benchmarks
  • Pricing recommendations
  • Contract-aware billing suggestions
  • Auto-approval suggestions with human review
  • Advanced finance workflow automation

7. Architecture Principles

  • Deterministic rules first: rely on source data and rules before advanced AI judgement.
  • AI as assistant: use AI for explanation, summarization, and support rather than as sole decision-maker.
  • Full audit trail: every result must show what data, rule, model, and approver were involved.
  • Normalized entities: standardize users, clients, projects, tasks, time entries, invoices, invoice lines, rate cards, and evidence events.

8. Recommended Beta Metrics

  • Percentage of time entries mapped to tasks
  • Percentage of hours verified versus flagged
  • Invoice draft generation time
  • Number of mismatches caught before invoicing
  • Human overrides of flagged entries
  • Finance review time per invoice cycle
